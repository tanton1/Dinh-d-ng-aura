# Aura Business Rules

- A contract must be effective on the session date and have remaining entitlement before a session can consume a paid session.
- Attendance and billing status are separate; an attendance update must not implicitly charge an exempt session.
- The schedule target of eight sessions per PT/day is a soft balancing target, never a hard capacity ceiling unless a separate policy says so.
- Schedule assignment priority is: pair an existing 1/2 slot, assigned official PT under the soft target, another suitable PT in the branch, then broader policy-approved options.
- A warning must identify whether a missing session is caused by contract/student constraints or PT capacity.
- Operational actions are redacted server-side and every state transition is auditable.
- AI may summarize or suggest; it cannot autonomously change a PT, charge a session, freeze a contract, collect money or renew a contract.
